The loop

  • You see the regime's army before you spend a credit — it is already standing on the board.
  • Drag a unit from the shop onto a hex to buy and place in one motion. Drag squads already down to rearrange; dropping one on another swaps them.
  • Squads that survive fight again next round, repaired. Squads wiped out are gone for good.
  • After each round you draft one reward of three and lose the rest. Rarer cards are exponentially stronger, and show up more the deeper the war runs — or when you are losing.

The counter web

  • Overkill is wasted. Damage lands on one machine at a time, so a 95-damage Colossus shot into a 63-hull Pod throws most of itself away. That is why cheap bodies work.
  • Splash hits several machines at once — devastating against a swarm, nearly pointless against one big target that only offers one body.
  • Armour is a save. Armour-piercing worsens that roll; at 7+ there is no save at all.
  • Range must be paid for. Siege reaches nine hexes but cannot fire inside three.

The board

  • Debris blocks line of sight and costs double to cross. Missile Tracks arc over it.
  • Ridges are high ground: +1 reach, −1 to hit shooting down, +1 shooting up. Climbing costs 3, moving along costs 2, dropping off costs 1.
